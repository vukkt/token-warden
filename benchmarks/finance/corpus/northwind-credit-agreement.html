AMENDED AND RESTATED CREDIT AGREEMENT

Dated as of March 14, 2024, among NORTHWIND LOGISTICS, INC., as Borrower, the Lenders party hereto, and MERIDIAN TRUST BANK, N.A., as Administrative Agent.

ARTICLE I. THE CREDITS

Section 1.01. Revolving Commitments

Each Lender severally agrees to make Revolving Loans to the Borrower from time to time during the Availability Period in an aggregate principal amount that will not result in the total Revolving Exposure exceeding 720.0 million (the "Aggregate Revolving Commitment").

Section 1.02. Maturity

The Revolving Commitments shall terminate, and all Revolving Loans shall be due and payable, on March 14, 2029 (the "Maturity Date").

Section 1.03. Interest

Revolving Loans shall bear interest at Term SOFR plus an Applicable Margin determined by reference to the Consolidated Leverage Ratio, ranging from 125 basis points to 225 basis points. As of the Closing Date the Applicable Margin is 150 basis points.

ARTICLE VI. FINANCIAL COVENANTS

Section 6.01. Consolidated Leverage Ratio

The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to exceed 3.75 to 1.00; provided that following a Material Acquisition the Borrower may elect to increase such maximum ratio to 4.25 to 1.00 for the four consecutive fiscal quarters thereafter.

Section 6.02. Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio

The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to be less than 3.00 to 1.00.

Section 6.03. Restricted Payments

The Borrower may make Restricted Payments, including share repurchases and dividends, so long as (a) no Default has occurred and is continuing and (b) the Consolidated Leverage Ratio on a pro forma basis does not exceed 3.25 to 1.00.

ARTICLE VII. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

An Event of Default shall occur upon, among other things, failure to pay principal when due, failure to pay interest within five Business Days of the due date, breach of any financial covenant set forth in Article VI, or a Change of Control.
