# Northwind Logistics, Inc. — Annual Report on Form 10-K

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024

All amounts in millions of U.S. dollars except per-share data and where otherwise noted.

## Item 1. Business

Northwind Logistics, Inc. ("Northwind," "we," "the Company") operates a
temperature-controlled freight network across North America and Western Europe.
The Company reports in three segments: Ground Freight, Cold Chain, and
Cross-Border. As of December 31, 2024 the Company operated 412 distribution
facilities and a fleet of 8,940 tractors.

The Company's customers are concentrated in grocery, pharmaceutical
distribution, and quick-service restaurant supply. Our ten largest customers
accounted for 38.2% of consolidated revenue in fiscal 2024, compared with 41.7%
in fiscal 2023. No single customer accounted for more than 10% of consolidated
revenue in either period.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

### Customer concentration

A loss of one or more of our ten largest customers, or a material reduction in
their shipping volumes, would adversely affect our results. Contracts with these
customers are generally terminable on 90 days' notice.

### Fuel price exposure

Diesel represented 14.6% of cost of revenue in fiscal 2024. We hedge
approximately 60% of anticipated diesel consumption on a rolling twelve-month
basis. A sustained $0.50 per gallon increase in diesel prices, unhedged, would
have increased cost of revenue by approximately 71.4 for fiscal 2024.

### Interest rate exposure

Borrowings under the Revolving Credit Facility bear interest at a floating rate.
A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in the applicable benchmark rate would
have increased annual interest expense by approximately 4.2 based on amounts
outstanding at December 31, 2024.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

### Results of operations

Consolidated revenue for fiscal 2024 was 4,182.6, an increase of 7.4% compared
with 3,894.3 in fiscal 2023. The increase was driven primarily by volume growth
in the Cold Chain segment and by contractual rate escalators, partially offset
by lower fuel surcharge revenue.

Cost of revenue was 3,301.9 in fiscal 2024 compared with 3,120.7 in fiscal 2023.
Gross profit was 880.7 in fiscal 2024 compared with 773.6 in fiscal 2023.

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was 431.5, representing an operating margin of
10.3%, compared with operating income of 355.1 and an operating margin of 9.1%
in fiscal 2023.

Net income attributable to Northwind was 268.4 in fiscal 2024 compared with
201.9 in fiscal 2023. Diluted earnings per share were 3.41 in fiscal 2024
compared with 2.56 in fiscal 2023.

Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was 702.8 in fiscal 2024 compared with
611.4 in fiscal 2023. A reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to net income appears
under "Non-GAAP financial measures" below.

### Segment results

Ground Freight revenue was 2,140.3 in fiscal 2024, an increase of 3.1% over
2,076.0 in fiscal 2023. Segment operating income was 188.7 compared with 171.2.

Cold Chain revenue was 1,388.9 in fiscal 2024, an increase of 16.2% over 1,195.3
in fiscal 2023. Segment operating income was 201.4 compared with 148.9. The
Company added 22 refrigerated facilities during fiscal 2024.

Cross-Border revenue was 653.4 in fiscal 2024, an increase of 3.3% over 623.0 in
fiscal 2023. Segment operating income was 41.4 compared with 35.0.

### Liquidity and capital resources

As of December 31, 2024 the Company held cash and cash equivalents of 512.7 and
had 420.0 of undrawn capacity under its Revolving Credit Facility, for total
available liquidity of 932.7. As of December 31, 2023 cash and cash equivalents
were 388.1.

Cash provided by operating activities was 596.2 in fiscal 2024 compared with
487.5 in fiscal 2023. Capital expenditures were 312.8 in fiscal 2024 compared
with 289.4 in fiscal 2023. Free cash flow, defined as cash provided by operating
activities less capital expenditures, was 283.4 in fiscal 2024.

Total debt outstanding at December 31, 2024 was 1,845.0, consisting of 1,545.0
of senior notes and 300.0 drawn under the Revolving Credit Facility. The ratio
of total debt to Adjusted EBITDA was 2.6x at December 31, 2024, compared with
3.0x at December 31, 2023.

During fiscal 2024 the Company repurchased 4.1 million shares of common stock
for total consideration of 214.0. The Board authorized an incremental 500.0
share repurchase program in November 2024, of which 500.0 remained available at
December 31, 2024.

### Non-GAAP financial measures

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income before interest, taxes, depreciation
and amortization, further adjusted to exclude restructuring charges, share-based
compensation, and gains or losses on asset disposals. For fiscal 2024, net
income of 268.4 is adjusted for interest expense of 96.3, income tax expense of
81.2, depreciation and amortization of 208.5, restructuring charges of 22.7,
share-based compensation of 31.4, and a gain on asset disposals of 5.7, yielding
Adjusted EBITDA of 702.8.
