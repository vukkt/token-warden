# Northwind Logistics, Inc. — Annual Report on Form 10-K

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023

All amounts in millions of U.S. dollars except per-share data and where otherwise noted.

## Item 1. Business

Northwind Logistics, Inc. operates a temperature-controlled freight network
across North America and Western Europe. The Company reports in three segments:
Ground Freight, Cold Chain, and Cross-Border. As of December 31, 2023 the
Company operated 390 distribution facilities and a fleet of 8,610 tractors.

Our ten largest customers accounted for 41.7% of consolidated revenue in fiscal
2023, compared with 44.9% in fiscal 2022.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

### Fuel price exposure

Diesel represented 16.1% of cost of revenue in fiscal 2023. We hedge
approximately 50% of anticipated diesel consumption on a rolling twelve-month
basis.

### Interest rate exposure

A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in the applicable benchmark rate would
have increased annual interest expense by approximately 5.5 based on amounts
outstanding at December 31, 2023.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

### Results of operations

Consolidated revenue for fiscal 2023 was 3,894.3, an increase of 6.5% compared
with 3,655.8 in fiscal 2022. Cost of revenue was 3,120.7 in fiscal 2023
compared with 2,971.4 in fiscal 2022.

Operating income for fiscal 2023 was 355.1, representing an operating margin of
9.1%, compared with operating income of 302.6 and an operating margin of 8.3% in
fiscal 2022.

Net income attributable to Northwind was 201.9 in fiscal 2023 compared with
168.3 in fiscal 2022. Diluted earnings per share were 2.56 in fiscal 2023
compared with 2.11 in fiscal 2022.

Adjusted EBITDA was 611.4 in fiscal 2023 compared with 540.2 in fiscal 2022.

### Segment results

Ground Freight revenue was 2,076.0 in fiscal 2023. Segment operating income was
171.2.

Cold Chain revenue was 1,195.3 in fiscal 2023. Segment operating income was
148.9.

Cross-Border revenue was 623.0 in fiscal 2023. Segment operating income was 35.0.

### Liquidity and capital resources

As of December 31, 2023 the Company held cash and cash equivalents of 388.1 and
had 250.0 of undrawn capacity under its Revolving Credit Facility, for total
available liquidity of 638.1.

Cash provided by operating activities was 487.5 in fiscal 2023. Capital
expenditures were 289.4 in fiscal 2023. Free cash flow was 198.1 in fiscal 2023.

Total debt outstanding at December 31, 2023 was 1,834.2. The ratio of total debt
to Adjusted EBITDA was 3.0x at December 31, 2023.

During fiscal 2023 the Company repurchased 2.2 million shares of common stock
for total consideration of 98.5.
